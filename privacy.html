3. Encryption, and exactly what it protects

This part matters, so it is written carefully rather than comfortably.

What is encrypted

When you sign up, your browser creates a random encryption key. That key is itself locked with a second key derived from your password using PBKDF2 with 310,000 rounds, and only the locked version is ever sent to us. Your password never leaves your device.

From then on, your message text, chat titles and profile notes are encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM before they are stored. What sits in our database is ciphertext. Neither we, nor our hosting providers, nor anyone who obtained a copy of the database, can read it without your password.

What is not encrypted, and you should know it

  • Your messages in flight to the AI. To answer you, your message and the recent conversation are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to OpenAI, where they are processed in readable form. Encryption at rest cannot change that. Do not type anything into any AI assistant that you would not be comfortable having processed by a third party.
  • Images you generate. These are stored as files and are not currently end-to-end encrypted.
  • Your email address, credit balance and payment records. These have to stay readable to run your account and meet our tax obligations.
If you forget your password, your chat history is gone. Not withheld, not recoverable by asking us nicely, gone. We do not hold a spare key, which is the entire point. Resetting your password gets you back into your account but not back into chats written before the reset.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase, for the database, accounts and file storage. Holds your encrypted chats and your email address.
  • Render, for running our server.
  • OpenAI, for generating replies and images. Receives your message text and any image you upload. OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Stripe, for payments. Stripe handles your card details directly. We never see or store your card number.
  • GitHub Pages, for serving the app itself.

Some of these process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the equivalent standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep things

  • Chats and images: until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Account records: until you delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Payment and tax records: six years, as UK law requires.
  • Technical logs: normally 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to processing, or ask for it in a portable form. Chats you export from within the app are already in a portable form, and are the readable copy.

Ask at privacy@nastiv.ee and we will answer within one month. If we get it wrong, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

One honest caveat on deletion requests. Because your chats are encrypted, we can delete them, but we cannot read them first to check what they contained. If you need a copy, export it before you delete.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The app keeps a small amount of data in your browser: your sign-in session, your encryption key for this device, your Fast or Smart preference, and, in guest mode, your chats. Clearing your browser data removes all of it, and on your next visit you will be asked for your password to unlock your chats again.

8. Children

Nastivee AI is not for under 18s. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Connections use TLS. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers, and your chat content is separately encrypted by you, as described above. Access to production systems is limited to our administrators. No system is perfect, and we will tell you and the ICO within 72 hours if a breach puts your data at risk.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating the date at the top.
