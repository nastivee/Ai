Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version. Your chats are encrypted in your browser with a key only your password can open, so we cannot read them. We also keep a second, separately encrypted copy so the assistant can remember what you told it before, and that one we can open. Your messages are still sent to OpenAI in readable form, because that is the only way a reply can be written. We do not sell anything to anybody.

3. Encryption, and exactly what it protects

This part matters, so it is written carefully rather than comfortably.

What is encrypted

When you sign up, your browser creates a random encryption key. That key is itself locked with a second key derived from your password using PBKDF2 with 310,000 rounds, and only the locked version is ever sent to us. Your password never leaves your device.

From then on, your message text, chat titles, profile notes and saved comments are encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM before they are stored. Anything you wrote before encryption was switched on is sealed the same way the next time you open the app. What sits in our database is ciphertext. Neither we, nor our hosting providers, nor anyone who obtained a copy of the database, can read it without your password.

What we keep so it can remember you

To answer a question about something you said weeks or months ago, the assistant needs a way to find that conversation again. Sending it your entire history every time would be slow and expensive, so instead we keep a separate searchable copy of each question and answer.

That copy is encrypted too, with AES-GCM, but with a key held on our server rather than the one derived from your password. It is ciphertext in the database, so a stolen copy of the database is no use on its own. The difference from the paragraph above is honest and worth stating plainly: we can open this copy, and the version encrypted with your password we cannot. It is opened only to find the few lines relevant to what you have just asked, and those lines are sent to the model along with your question.

Deleting a chat deletes what was remembered from it. Deleting your account deletes all of it.

What is not encrypted, and you should know it

  • Pictures and clips. These are kept as ordinary JPEG, PNG, WebP or MP4 files in private storage that only your signed in account can fetch. They are protected by that access control, not by your password. Pictures saved before 22 September 2026 stay encrypted.
  • Photos you upload. Every photo you upload, to edit it, ask about it or start a video from it, is kept, including uploads made without an account. Our team can view uploads to keep the service safe and deal with misuse, and can remove any that break our terms. You can see your own under My artwork, and deleting your account deletes them.
  • Your messages in flight to the AI. To answer you, your message and the recent conversation are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to OpenAI, where they are processed in readable form. Encryption at rest cannot change that. Do not type anything into any AI assistant that you would not be comfortable having processed by a third party.
  • Pictures on their way to be made or edited. Like your messages, a picture you ask to change is sent to OpenAI in readable form so the change can be made.
  • Your email address, credit balance and payment records. These have to stay readable to run your account and meet our tax obligations.

Your recovery code

When your encryption is set up, you are given a recovery code. It is a second key to your chats that you hold and we never see. Changing your password from inside the app keeps your chats, because the encryption key itself does not change, only the lock on it.

If you forget your password and lose your recovery code, your chat history is gone. Not withheld, not recoverable by asking us nicely, gone. We do not hold a spare key, which is the entire point. Keep the recovery code somewhere safe and away from the device you use.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase, for the database, accounts and file storage. Holds your encrypted chats and your email address.
  • Render, for running our server.
  • OpenAI, for generating replies and images. Receives your message text and any image you upload. OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Stripe, for payments. Stripe handles your card details directly. We never see or store your card number.
  • GitHub Pages, for serving the app itself.

Some of these process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the equivalent standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep things

  • Chats and images: until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Account records: until you delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Payment records: held by Stripe for as long as UK tax law requires. Our own record of your credits is deleted with your account.
  • Technical logs: normally 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to processing, or ask for it in a portable form. Chats you export from within the app are already in a portable form, and are the readable copy.

You can export all your chats, and delete your account and everything in it, yourself, from My profile in the app. It takes effect straight away.

For anything else, ask at privacy@heyatla.co.uk and we will answer within one month. If we get it wrong, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

One honest caveat on deletion requests. Because your chats are encrypted, we can delete them, but we cannot read them first to check what they contained. If you need a copy, export it before you delete.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The app keeps a small amount of data in your browser: your sign-in session, your encryption key for this device, your Fast or Smart preference, and, in guest mode, your chats. Clearing your browser data removes all of it, and on your next visit you will be asked for your password to unlock your chats again.

8. Children

Atla is not for under 18s. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Connections use TLS. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers, and your chat content is separately encrypted by you, as described above. Access to production systems is limited to our administrators. No system is perfect, and we will tell you and the ICO within 72 hours if a breach puts your data at risk.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating the date at the top.
